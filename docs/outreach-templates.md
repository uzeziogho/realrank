# RealRank founder outreach — DM & post templates

The #1 bottleneck isn't code — it's a full board. These are copy-paste templates
to recruit the first ~30–50 sites. Personalize the first line every time; the
worst thing you can send is something that reads like a mass blast.

**Golden rules**
- Lead with *them*, not you. Reference their site/tweet/launch specifically.
- Give before you ask — a compliment, their momentum number, a spot reserved.
- One clear ask, one link. Never two.
- Founding scarcity is your leverage while the board is empty: `realrank.lol/founding`.

---

## 1. Cold DM — indie founder who ships in public

> Hey {name} — been watching {product} climb, congrats on the {recent launch/milestone}.
>
> I'm building RealRank: a leaderboard of sites ranked by **verified** organic
> traffic (real Google Search Console clicks, not SimilarWeb guesses). No
> pay-to-rank — growth decides the order.
>
> It's brand new, so the first 50 sites are founding members (permanent badge,
> easy climb while it's quiet). Want me to save {product} a founding spot?
> Takes ~30s to connect, read-only. → realrank.lol/founding

## 2. Cold DM — warmer, you know their numbers are good

## 3. Reply to a "just hit X visitors / launched" tweet

> Love this. If you want that traffic *verified* and on a public board (GSC, not
> estimates — nobody can pad it), RealRank's founding spots are open:
> realrank.lol/founding — you'd rank well.

## 4. The "grade" hook (uses the free tool — no ask yet)

> ranks on.)

_Then, if they engage:_ "Board's new — want a founding spot so it's your verified
grade, ranked publicly? realrank.lol/founding"

## Where to find the first 50

- People replying to launch tweets ("just shipped", "hit $X MRR", "X visitors").
- Indie Hackers / r/SaaS / build-in-public communities.
- Directories your audience already uses (tinylaunch, tinyshelf — your current
  referrers) — the same crowd.
- Anyone who already tried a free tool (momentum-score / growth-grade / is-my-traffic-real)
  is warm — reach out.

## Do / don't

- **Do** send 5–10 personalized DMs a day, not 100 blasts.
- **Do** reserve a spot ("saved you #12") — reciprocity + scarcity.
- **Don't** promise traffic *to* their site — RealRank sends some referral clicks,
  but the pitch is proof + discovery, not a traffic firehose. Stay honest; it's the brand.
